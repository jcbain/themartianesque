It's been a minute since I've written for myself. You may be saying, "but James, this is a blog and the whole world has access to it. It's a stretch to call it 'for yourself'." To that one reader out there holding on to this conviction I only have 2 things to say to you. 1. Thank you. I'm happy that you've found my little corner of the internet and flattered you'd think that there was more than just you reading this thing. My mother wouldn't even read this. And 2. Blogs are always personal to me. I use them as a distillation step in the process of my thinking. It's only fortuitous for you, dear individual reader, that I'm opening a window into my brain for you to bear witness. You're welcome and be careful.

Now what was I on about before I got off track? Oh that's right. I'm back to writing a blog. It comes at the heels of diving into parenthood (my son is 10 weeks at the writing of this post) and my thoughts on the world haven't ever been more intentional (albeit a bit more hazy and probably full of spit up. Spit up is everywhere and I doubt even my thoughts are safe.) And quite frankly, I want to show him a world that is better than the world we currently find ourselves in (side eyeing you, America). So here we are, you and me and this blog and my thoughts.

But why? Well, I miss the days when the computer was fun. Nowadays it's all TikTok and smut (not that those don't have their place) but what happened to Homestar Runner? I miss the days when the weirdest people took to their blogs to carve out their little slice of the World Wide Web. And I figured if I miss it then why not bring it back… for me. I didn't say that these past 10 weeks were ones of lucidity. But I want a place to document this journey because there already feels like there is a lot to say and I can only imagine that these coming years will contain a lot more.
So what is this blog? I don't know. I only work here. But seriously, I imagine it will be part some random/not so random musings, part exposé into some fun projects we're working on with a little splash of my professional and academic interests. In other words, we'll see when we get there.

However, if you need something a bit more sinewy to sink your teeth into, I imagine myself tackling topics relating to me personally. This could include my research into local (as in Calgary, AB local) happenings, topics on evolutionary linguistics, or even the occasional discussion about some garbage reality television show because I just can't help myself. Parenting woes are bound to sneak in as well. I wouldn't be surprised if this turned into a fully virulent mommy blog.

I guess I should say welcome to the kitchen sink that is my brain. Better, more marketable souls would probably have a tighter focus. Something insightful about something specific. I'm not blessed with a brain like that. Even if I had such a narrow focus, and I'm not pretending to say that I ever did, I'd find something else shiny to distract my attention. My cohesive idea is my lack thereof and I like it this way. I find it to embody the spirit of what blogging originally set out to be. Like a middle school journal; only one where I foolishly make copies of and, by some divine lack of judgment, disseminate them throughout the halls known to you and me as the internet.

This brings me to the last topic of discussion and one in which I am sure you've been scratching your noggin over, dear reader. Why is this blog called The Martianesque? Am I some Elon stan hoping he'll see my AIM handle and pick me first to inhabit Mars? I don't put it past his fragile little ego to go about selecting candidates in such a manner. But no. That's not it. Also, it's funny to pretend we live in a world in which Elon will actually get people to Mars. Nah, the real reason is because I often struggle with feeling normal and yet I know that I am still human. In a way, I'm kind of an outsider (a Martian if you will) but only slightly (esque).

To be honest, I'm not quite sure how much the feeling of being bizarre will come into my writing or not. I imagine in many ways it informs who I am and therefore will inevitably wiggle its way onto the screen intentionally or otherwise. We'll see.

But without further ado, welcome to the Martianesque! I'm happy you're here!

— James
