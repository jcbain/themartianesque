There is a specific kind of hunger you only learn from watching Survivor at 11pm with bad posture and good intentions. It's not hunger for food. It's hunger for a middle-aged man in a buff to weep over a Snickers bar like he just saw his childhood home demolished.

The show is anthropology cosplay with worse hygiene and better confessionals. Jeff Probst has the energy of a camp counselor who read one Malcolm Gladwell book and never recovered. I respect that.

What I can't get over is how seriously everyone takes the merge feast. Tribal council is chess. The reward challenge is a medieval banquet where someone always cries into a coconut. That's television. That's art if you're very tired.

I've tried to explain to my partner why "Boston Rob" constitutes a literary archetype. She left the room. Correct response, honestly. This essay belongs nowhere near parenting posts or linguistics musings, which is why it was dragged to the Trash with dignity intact and crumbs on its shirt.

Future archaeologists will find this file and assume our civilization worshipped torches and peanut butter. They won't be entirely wrong.
